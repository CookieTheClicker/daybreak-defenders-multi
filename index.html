    Tips & Info

    • Structures: Use 1-8 or the mouse wheel to choose an inventory slot. Selecting a structure kit readies it for placement.
    • Left click to place, press R to rotate turrets, Esc cancels placement when one is selected.
    • Press Esc or P to open the pause menu.
    • Craft kits at the house crafting table before placing structures.
    • Toggle inventory with I, upgrade nearby structures with F, gather resources with E.
    • Hold Shift to sprint while moving.
    • Drop replacement art in assets/props and update the loader in src/assets.js.

    Controls

    • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
    • Interact or gather with E.
    • Attack with Space.
    • Upgrade nearby structures with F.
    • Toggle the inventory with I.
    • Select inventory slots with 1-8 or the mouse wheel.
    • Rotate turret placement with R.
    • Pause or resume with Esc or P.
    • Open loot chests with E to discover gear.
